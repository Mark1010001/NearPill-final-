Download the NearPill Android app

Find medicines at pharmacies near you, reserve them, and pick them up. This is the demo version for Android phones.

Download for Android (.apk)

About 175 MB. If the download does not start, open the download page.

How to install

  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download for Android.
  2. When the download finishes, tap the file NearPill.apk in your notifications or in the Downloads folder.
  3. If your phone says it can't install apps from this source, tap Settings and allow installs from your browser or Files app, then go back and tap the file again.
  4. Tap Install, then Open. Create an account or sign in.

Good to know

  • This version is for demonstration and is not on the Google Play Store. Android shows a warning for any app installed from outside the Play Store.
  • The app needs an internet connection. Allow location when asked to see pharmacies near you. Your location is not stored on our servers.
